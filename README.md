Public QR Gallery
Concept

Public QR Gallery is an open, location-based digital art platform that connects physical public space with curated digital exhibitions.

By scanning QR codes placed in real-world locations, people can instantly access and explore artwork — turning streets into distributed, evolving galleries.

How It Works
Discovery
A person encounters a QR code placed in a public space (street, wall, object)
Access
Scanning the QR code opens a digital artwork (2D, space-aware composition)
Exploration
The artwork is designed to be:
visually immersive
optimized for mobile viewing
aware of its placement context
Gallery Experience
A single street or area can represent:
one artist
one theme
one curated collection
Rotation
Artists and exhibitions change over time, creating a dynamic, evolving public gallery

Vision
Transform cities into open-access art spaces
Remove barriers between artists and public
Combine physical movement with digital discovery
Enable temporary, flexible exhibitions without infrastructure

System Design (Conceptual)
QR Layer
Physical QR codes placed in public locations
Each QR links to a specific artwork or gallery entry
Content Layer
Hosted images / artwork (optimized for mobile)
Structured per artist / collection
Gallery Logic
Mapping:
location → artist
QR → artwork
Ability to rotate content over time
User Interaction
Instant access (no app required)
lightweight web experience

Exhibition Model
Single Street = Single Artist
Multiple QR points = multiple artworks
Time-based rotation:
weekly / monthly artist changes
seasonal themes
curated events

Current Status
Prototype / early implementation
Testing:
QR placement strategy
user interaction behavior
accessibility in public environments

Future Ideas
Artist profiles and storytelling
Audio layers (artist commentary)
AI-assisted curation or generative art
Location-aware content (time, weather, events)
Community submissions

Why This Matters

This project explores:

real-world + digital system integration
decentralized content delivery
human interaction with ambient technology

It is not just a gallery — it is a platform for public digital experience.
